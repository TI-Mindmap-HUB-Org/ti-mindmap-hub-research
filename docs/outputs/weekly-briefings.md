# Weekly Briefings

TI Mindmap HUB produces a weekly threat intelligence briefing using a multi-agent AI system that aggregates and synthesizes the week's processed reports.

---

## Multi-Agent Architecture

The briefing is generated by five specialized agents working in sequence:

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Collector** | Aggregates all reports processed during the week |
| **Analyst** | Each analyst reviews a subset of reports for key findings |
| **Trend** | Identifies patterns and emerging themes across all analyses |
| **Synthesis** | Produces the final structured briefing |
| **Editor** | Reviews and refines the output for clarity and accuracy |

---

## Briefing Sections

Each weekly briefing includes:

- **Executive Summary** — High-level overview of the week's threat landscape
- **Top TTPs Observed** — Most frequently mapped MITRE ATT&CK techniques
- **Most Targeted Sectors** — Industries and sectors appearing most in reports
- **Emerging Threats** — New or escalating threat activity
- **Notable Campaigns** — Deep dives into significant campaigns from the week

---

## Accessing Briefings

- **Web interface** — Visit [ti-mindmap-hub.com](https://ti-mindmap-hub.com) and navigate to the Briefings section
- **MCP tools** — Use `get_latest_briefing`, `list_briefings`, or `get_briefing_by_date`
- **API** — Retrieve via authenticated API calls

---

## Known Limitations

- **Sample bias** — Trends reflect only the sources monitored, not the entire threat landscape
- **Recency bias** — More recent reports may be weighted more heavily
- **Oversimplification** — Complex multi-stage campaigns may be condensed
- **Subjective prioritization** — "Most significant" assessments are inherently subjective

See [Known Limitations](../concepts/limitations.md#weekly-briefing-limitations) for details.
