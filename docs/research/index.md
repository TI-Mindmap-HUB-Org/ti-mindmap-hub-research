# Research

TI Mindmap HUB is an independent research project exploring the intersection of Generative AI and Cyber Threat Intelligence. This section documents academic partnerships, research outcomes, and collaboration opportunities.

---

## Research Objectives

- Evaluate LLM accuracy in automated IOC extraction
- Explore STIX 2.1 bundle generation from unstructured text
- Map TTPs to the MITRE ATT&CK framework programmatically
- Understand limitations and failure modes of AI in security contexts
- Study human-AI collaboration in threat analysis workflows

---

## Active Research Areas

| Area | Topics |
|------|--------|
| **STIX 2.1 generation** | Multi-language support, extraction accuracy, validation |
| **Knowledge graphs** | Entity resolution, temporal analysis, graph-based hunting |
| **LLM evaluation** | Benchmarking, hallucination detection, adversarial testing |
| **MCP applications** | Novel tools for security, multi-agent systems |
